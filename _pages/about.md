I am an AI researcher working at the intersection of **machine learning, causal inference, and healthcare**, with a PhD in Data and Decisions Sciences from the Technion, advised by Prof. Uri Shalit and Prof. Danny Eytan.

My research background includes:

- **Zebra Medical Vision:** Computer vision and deep learning models for predicting clinical findings in CT scans.
- **Weizmann Institute of Science, Segal Lab:** COVID-19 response research, applying prediction and causal inference methods to tabular and time-to-event data.
- **PhD research, Technion:** Heterogeneous treatment-effect estimation, survival analysis, and physical AI — hybrid mechanistic models that combine scientific knowledge with learned dynamics.

I am interested in innovative ideas that connect research with practical products and advance personalized medicine. I work with a variety of data modalities, including patients' electronic health records, longitudinal time series, survival data, and medical images.

Explore my [Selected research](#research) summaries below. Each project has a dedicated page with its motivation, example applications, findings, and links to the papers and code.

## Selected research
{: #research}

**Select a project title to read its full summary.**

- **[Hybrid models for intervention outcomes](/projects-hybrid/)** — Combining mechanistic models with neural networks to predict responses to time-dependent interventions, including under distribution shift. *Preprint, 2026.*
- **[Personalized treatment effects with censored outcomes](/projects-hte-tte/)** — MISTR estimates how treatment benefits vary across patients when follow-up is incomplete. *ICML, 2025.*
- **[Survival analysis and PyDTS](/projects-pydts/)** — Efficient methods and open-source tools for discrete-time outcomes with competing events, with an application to ICU length of stay. *Biometrics and JOSS, 2025.*
- **[COVID-19 response](/projects-covid/)** — Modeling hospital demand and mortality, alongside national symptom surveillance and vaccine-impact research. *Nature Communications and Nature Medicine, 2021.*
- **[Fracture detection in CT](/projects-vcf/)** — Deep learning for identifying vertebral compression fractures in routine imaging. *MICCAI, 2020.*
