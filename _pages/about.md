I am an AI researcher working at the intersection of **machine learning, causal inference, and healthcare**.

I completed my PhD at the Technion's Faculty of Data and Decision Sciences under the supervision of Prof. Uri Shalit and Prof. Danny Eytan.

My research background includes:

- **Zebra Medical Vision Ltd.:** Prediction of clinical findings in CT scans using computer vision and deep learning.
- **Segal Lab, Weizmann Institute of Science:** COVID-19 response research, applying prediction and causal inference methods to tabular and time-to-event data.
- **PhD, Technion:** Heterogeneous treatment-effect estimation in time-to-event data, survival analysis, and physical AI — hybrid mechanistic–data-driven models that combine scientific knowledge with deep learning to model system dynamics.

I have experience with a variety of data modalities, including patients' electronic health records, longitudinal time series, survival data, and medical imaging.

## Selected research
{: #research}

**Select a project title to read more.**

- **[Hybrid models for intervention outcomes](/projects-hybrid/)** — Combining mechanistic models with neural networks to predict responses to time-dependent interventions, including under distribution shift. *Preprint, 2026.*
- **[Personalized treatment effects with censored outcomes](/projects-hte-tte/)** — MISTR estimates how treatment benefits vary across patients when follow-up is incomplete. *ICML, 2025.*
- **[Survival analysis and PyDTS](/projects-pydts/)** — Efficient methods and open-source tools for discrete-time outcomes with competing events, with an application to ICU length of stay. *Biometrics and JOSS, 2025.*
- **[COVID-19 response](/projects-covid/)** — National symptom surveys to identify outbreak hotspots and prioritize testing, modeling of hospital demand and mortality, and studies of population-level vaccine impact. *Nature Communications and Nature Medicine, 2021.*
- **[Fracture detection in CT](/projects-vcf/)** — Deep learning for identifying vertebral compression fractures in routine CT imaging. *MICCAI, 2020.*
