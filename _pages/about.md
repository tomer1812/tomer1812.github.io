I am an AI researcher working at the intersection of **machine learning, causal inference, and healthcare**. My work asks how we can move from predicting patient outcomes to understanding which interventions could improve them.

I hold a PhD in Data and Decisions Sciences from the Technion, advised by Prof. Uri Shalit and Prof. Danny Eytan. My research spans heterogeneous treatment-effect estimation, survival analysis, and hybrid mechanistic models that combine scientific knowledge with learned dynamics.

<!-- research -->

## Selected research
{: #research}

- **[Hybrid models for intervention outcomes](/projects-hybrid/)** — Combining mechanistic models with neural networks to predict responses to time-dependent interventions, including under distribution shift. *Preprint, 2026.*
- **[Personalized treatment effects with censored outcomes](/projects-hte-tte/)** — MISTR estimates how treatment benefits vary across patients when follow-up is incomplete. *ICML, 2025.*
- **[Survival analysis and PyDTS](/projects-pydts/)** — Efficient methods and open-source tools for discrete-time outcomes with competing events, with an application to ICU length of stay. *Biometrics and JOSS, 2025.*
- **[COVID-19 response](/projects-covid/)** — Modeling hospital demand and mortality, alongside national symptom surveillance and vaccine-impact research. *Nature Communications and Nature Medicine, 2021.*
- **[Fracture detection in CT](/projects-vcf/)** — Deep learning for identifying vertebral compression fractures in routine imaging. *MICCAI, 2020.*

## Background

At the Weizmann Institute of Science's Segal Lab, I developed models and analyses to support the COVID-19 response. Earlier, at Zebra Medical Vision, I worked on deep-learning systems for medical imaging. I also served as head lecturer for Machine Learning 1 at the Technion.

I am interested in translating research into practical tools for personalized medicine, working with electronic health records, longitudinal time series, survival data, and medical images.
