At the **Segal Lab, Weizmann Institute of Science (2020–2021)**, I developed models and analyses to support public-health decisions during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## Hospital load and mortality

We combined multistate survival modeling with nationwide records of **22,636 hospitalized patients** to study how hospital burden related to mortality. Periods with more than 500 severe or critical patients hospitalized nationally were associated with higher mortality than lower-load periods, even after accounting for differences in patient characteristics.
